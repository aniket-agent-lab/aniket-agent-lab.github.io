Life Agent

A private personal assistant, used by one person: its owner.

Life Agent is a self-hosted assistant that its owner talks to through a private Telegram bot. It runs on the owner's own server. There is no sign-up, no other users, and nothing is offered to the public.

What it does

  • Sends a morning brief and reminders, and keeps a task list.
  • Reads the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar to prepare those briefs.
  • Logs the owner's own expenses to a Google Sheet, from bank alert SMS and emails.
  • Adds events to the owner's Google Calendar when the owner asks.
  • Creates and edits documents in the owner's Google Drive when the owner asks.
