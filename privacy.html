Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Life Agent is a personal, self-hosted assistant with a single user: its owner, Aniket Shevade. It is not offered to anyone else, and no other person's data is processed. Google account data is accessed only for the owner's own account, at the owner's request.

Google data this app uses

Google permissionWhat it is used for
Gmail (read, send, modify)Summarising the owner's unread mail in a daily brief, reading the owner's bank transaction alerts to log expenses, and sending or drafting mail that the owner explicitly asks for.
Google CalendarReading the owner's schedule for briefs and conflict warnings, and creating events the owner asks for.
Google SheetsWriting the owner's expense records, balances and budgets to a spreadsheet in the owner's own Drive.
Google Docs and DriveCreating and editing documents the owner asks for, in the owner's own Drive.
Google Apps ScriptCreating and updating automation scripts in the owner's own account, at the owner's request.
Contacts (read-only)Resolving names the owner refers to when drafting mail or invitations.

How data is handled

  • Where it runs: on a private server rented and controlled by the owner. Access tokens are stored there in files readable only by the owner's account.
  • Storage: the app stores only what the owner asks it to keep — tasks, expense records and its own notes. Emails and calendar entries are read when needed and are not copied into a separate database.
  • Sharing: data is never sold, rented or shared for advertising. Nothing is shared with any other person.
  • AI processing: when the owner asks a question, the relevant text may be sent to a third-party AI provider (currently OpenRouter and the model providers it routes to) to produce an answer. Providers are configured to exclude prompts from training where that option exists.
  • Retention and deletion: the owner can delete any stored record at any time, and can revoke this app's access to their Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends all access.
  • Limited Use: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
